# rf-model

Training a new radio path-loss prediction model.

The goal is a model that predicts signal loss over a 50 km × 50 km area at
30 m resolution, across a wide frequency range (VHF through X-band, roughly
30 MHz to 12 GHz). The previous model covered a 3.84 km tile at cellular
frequencies.

Training runs on Purdue's Anvil cluster.

This repository is just getting started.
